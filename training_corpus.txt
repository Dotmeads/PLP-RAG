## Usage Notes

This corpus is specifically designed for answering pet care questions, with emphasis on:
- **Nutrition and feeding** (largest category)
- **Veterinary guidelines and standards**
- **Health and wellness information**
- **Breed-specific care information**

The RAG system uses this corpus to provide evidence-based, accurate responses to pet care questions by retrieving relevant information and generating contextual answers.
